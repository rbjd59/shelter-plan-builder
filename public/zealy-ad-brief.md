## 2. What we are promoting
A free, pro bono pre-detention defense plan for immigrant working families in the United States. Users sign up on the website, receive an activation code and a download link for the mobile app, and — if they are ever detained by ICE — can press one SOS button to alert family and their attorney. A Florida-licensed immigration attorney reviews the federal documents (AO 242 habeas petition, IFP fee waiver, etc.) that are prepared for their case.

## 3. Audience
- Immigrant working families worried about ICE detention
- Spanish, English, and Haitian Creole speakers
- Homeowners who want to protect their property
- Employers, churches, and community groups helping families prepare
- Geographic focus: Florida and U.S. immigrant communities

## 4. Key benefits
- **Free during the community crisis** — normally a paid service; currently offered pro bono through the Refuge Outreach community program.
- **One-button SOS** — alerts family and attorney instantly if detained.
- **Attorney-reviewed** — documents are reviewed by Sorrentino Law Firm, PLLC, a Florida-licensed immigration law firm.
- **Bilingual / trilingual** — fully available in Spanish, English, and Haitian Creole.
- **Private** — information is protected under attorney-client privilege.
- **Prepared before it happens** — federal forms and a defense plan are ready before an arrest occurs.

## 5. Key features
- Mobile app with red SOS button and “I’m Safe” cancellation PIN
- Activation code sent by email/SMS after signup
- Federal form packet (AO 242, JS-44, IFP waiver, memorandum, motion referral)
- Attorney/company board for case tracking and document editing
- Locate workflow to capture facility, warden, A-number, and mailing address after activation
- Homeowner protection video and low-cost trust information
- Employer / church program
