## VEHICLE RESCUE AGREEMENT (Don't Lose Your Car / No Pierdas Tu Carro / Pa Pèdi Machin Ou)

Parties. This Vehicle Rescue Agreement ("Agreement") is between DETENCIONDEFENSACOM, INC., a Delaware corporation doing business as DetencionDefensa.com and Don't Lose Your Car ("Company"), and the vehicle owner(s) signing below ("Owner"). If more than one person is named on the title, every titled owner must sign, and "Owner" means all of them.

1. What this program does. If the Owner's vehicle is towed, impounded, or must be sold because the Owner is detained or leaving the United States, the Company will (a) find out where the vehicle is held, (b) calculate whether a sale would leave money for the Owner, and (c) if it would, refer the vehicle to a licensed Florida motor vehicle dealer ("Partner Dealer") that has agreed to retrieve it and buy it at no less than the Guaranteed Minimum Price in Schedule A.

2. What the Company is not. The Company is not a motor vehicle dealer and does not buy, sell, or hold title to vehicles. It does not receive, hold, or transmit the Owner's money. It does not give immigration, criminal, or tax advice. Documents prepared through the program are reviewed by Sorrentino Law Firm, PLLC under that firm's separate limited-scope engagement with the Owner.

3. Referral fee disclosure. The Partner Dealer pays the Company a referral fee of $250 for each vehicle the Partner Dealer accepts. The Owner pays the Company nothing. This fee is not deducted from the Owner's proceeds and does not reduce the Guaranteed Minimum Price.

4. Trigger events. The Agent named in Schedule C may act only after one of these events ("Trigger Event"): (1) the Owner is taken into immigration or criminal custody; (2) the vehicle is towed or impounded while the Owner cannot retrieve it; (3) the Owner, or the Owner's Designated Contact, asks in writing (text or app message counts) for the vehicle to be sold; or (4) the Owner departs or is removed from the United States. The powers of attorney signed with this Agreement are effective when signed, as Florida law requires. The limits on when the Agent may act are a binding instruction between the Owner and the Agent and are set out in Schedule C.

5. Viability test. No pickup unless the Owner nets money. Before any retrieval, the Company will calculate: Estimated Net to Owner = Guaranteed Minimum Price − towing and storage charges through the expected pickup date − any lien payoff − the dealer's transport cost listed in Schedule A. If the Estimated Net to Owner is less than $500 (the "Minimum Net"), the vehicle will not be retrieved under this program. The Company will tell the Designated Contact in writing, show the calculation, and explain the other options, such as voluntary surrender to the lender.

6. Retrieval. If the viability test is met, the Partner Dealer may pay the towing and storage charges and retrieve the vehicle using the Authorization to Release Impounded Vehicle. If a law enforcement agency has placed a hold on the vehicle, retrieval waits until the agency releases it. The Partner Dealer advances the charges. They are deducted only as shown in the Schedule A settlement statement.

7. Inspection and offer. Within 24 hours after retrieval, the Partner Dealer will send the Designated Contact dated photos, a condition report, the benchmark valuation printout, and a written offer of at least the Guaranteed Minimum Price.

8. Right to refuse the offer. The Owner or Designated Contact may reject the offer in writing within 48 hours of receiving it. If the offer is rejected, the Partner Dealer will release the vehicle to the person the Owner names once the charges it advanced are repaid. Silence after 48 hours counts as acceptance only if the offer is at or above the Guaranteed Minimum Price.

9. Liens. The Owner authorizes the Agent and the Partner Dealer to request a payoff statement from any lienholder and to pay off the lien out of the sale price, but only if the Minimum Net is still met after payoff.

10. Payment of proceeds. The Partner Dealer pays the net proceeds directly to the payee in Schedule B within 3 business days of title transfer, by check, ACH, or deposit to the detainee's commissary account through the facility's approved deposit service.

11. Title, plates, odometer, and insurance. The Agent signs the title, bill of sale (HSMV 82050), and odometer disclosure for the Owner. The Partner Dealer removes the license plate and returns it to the Designated Contact or surrenders it as Florida law requires. The Owner is responsible for cancelling insurance after the sale.

12. Personal property. Personal items found in the vehicle are photographed, inventoried, and held for the Designated Contact for 30 days. Items left after that may be disposed of as allowed by law. Weapons, cash over $100, and documents are reported to the Designated Contact right away.

13. Condition and liability. The Owner understands that the vehicle may have been damaged before it was towed or while it was in storage. Neither the Company nor the Partner Dealer is responsible for damage that occurred before the Partner Dealer took possession. The Partner Dealer is responsible for the vehicle while it is in the Partner Dealer's possession.

14. No guarantees beyond Schedule A. The Company does not promise that a sale will happen or that any price beyond the Guaranteed Minimum Price will be paid. The Company makes no statement about whether it is safe for any person to visit a tow yard or government office.

15. Revocation. The Owner may cancel this Agreement and the related powers of attorney at any time before a sale closes, using Schedule D or any signed writing. Cancellation does not undo a sale that has already closed, and the Owner must repay any charges the Partner Dealer advanced before the vehicle is released.

16. Privacy. The Company collects only the information needed to locate, value, and transfer the vehicle. It does not ask about or record immigration status for this program. It shares information only with the Partner Dealer, the Agent, the tow operator, the lienholder, and government motor vehicle offices, as needed to carry out this Agreement.

17. Language. This Agreement is provided in English, with courtesy translations in Spanish and Haitian Kreyòl. The English version controls.

18. Governing law and disputes. Florida law governs. Disputes go to small claims or county court in Miami-Dade County, Florida. Nothing in this Agreement waives a consumer right under Florida or federal law.

19. Entire agreement. This Agreement, its Schedules A–D, and the state forms signed with it are the whole agreement for this program.

## SCHEDULE A — Guaranteed Minimum Price and settlement statement

The Guaranteed Minimum Price is the wholesale value from the Benchmark on the day of retrieval, adjusted only for condition items that are photographed and listed in the inspection report. Benchmark: a recognized wholesale auction valuation guide (such as Manheim Market Report or J.D. Power wholesale), with the VIN, mileage, and region entered. Condition adjustments allowed: documented body damage, missing keys, non-running vehicle, flood or fire damage, missing catalytic converter, and anything else listed with photos; each must show its dollar amount. Not allowed: dealer profit margin, undocumented reconditioning, or the referral fee. Dealer transport cost (fixed): USD ____ within Miami-Dade and Broward; USD ____ elsewhere in Florida.

Settlement statement table: Benchmark wholesale value; Less documented condition adjustments; Guaranteed Minimum Price / offer (if higher); Less towing and storage advanced by dealer; Less lien payoff; Less fixed transport cost; Net to Owner (must be at least the Minimum Net).

## DEALER PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT

Parties: DETENCIONDEFENSACOM, INC., doing business as DetencionDefensa.com and Don't Lose Your Car ("Company"), and ____, a licensed Florida motor vehicle dealer, license no. ____ ("Dealer").

1. Referrals. The Company may refer vehicles whose owners have signed a Vehicle Rescue Agreement. A referral includes the vehicle information, the owner's signed authorizations, and the Company's viability calculation. No minimum number of referrals is guaranteed.

2. Response times. Within 4 business hours of a referral, the Dealer accepts or declines it. After accepting, the Dealer retrieves the vehicle within 48 hours, or sooner if the tow yard is about to sell it, unless a law enforcement hold prevents retrieval.

3. Guaranteed Minimum Price. The Dealer's offer will be at least the benchmark wholesale value under Schedule A of the owner's Vehicle Rescue Agreement, adjusted only for documented condition items. The Dealer sends photos, the condition report, the benchmark printout, and the settlement statement to the owner's Designated Contact within 24 hours of retrieval.

4. Owner's right to refuse. If the owner rejects the offer, the Dealer releases the vehicle to the person the owner names once the towing and storage charges the Dealer advanced are repaid. The Dealer charges no storage of its own for the first 10 days after rejection and no more than USD ____ per day after that.

5. Advanced charges. The Dealer pays towing, storage, and lien payoff amounts up front and recovers them only as shown on the settlement statement, with receipts attached.

6. Referral fee. The Dealer pays the Company USD 250 for each referred vehicle the Dealer accepts, within 10 days after the end of each month, with a monthly report by VIN. No fee is owed on a referral the Dealer declines or that does not close because of the viability test or the owner's rejection. The fee may not be deducted from, or passed on to, the owner in any form.

7. Payment to owner. The Dealer pays the net proceeds directly to the payee in the owner's Schedule B within 3 business days of title transfer. The Dealer never pays proceeds to the Company.

8. Title work. The Dealer handles title transfer, odometer disclosure, and plate removal in compliance with Florida law, and gives the Company and the Designated Contact copies of the completed title documents.

9. Fair dealing. The Dealer will not: ask about, record, or disclose any owner's immigration status; contact any government agency about an owner except as required for title or vehicle release; offer an owner or family member any side deal, loan, or trade outside this program; resell or share owner personal information; or use the names "DetencionDefensa", "Don't Lose Your Car", or "No Pierdas Tu Carro" in advertising without the Company's written approval.

10. Licensing, insurance, compliance. The Dealer keeps its Florida dealer license, surety bond, and garage liability insurance of at least USD 1,000,000 per occurrence naming the Company as additional insured; complies with all Florida motor vehicle, towing, consumer protection, and federal odometer laws; and notifies the Company within 2 business days of any license action.

11. Audit. The Company may audit any referred transaction, including benchmark printouts and resale records, on 5 business days' notice. If an audit shows the owner was paid less than the Guaranteed Minimum Price, the Dealer pays the shortfall to the owner within 10 days, plus 10%.

12. Indemnity. The Dealer indemnifies the Company and the owner against claims arising from the Dealer's handling, storage, transport, title work, or resale of a referred vehicle.

13. Independent parties; no exclusivity. The Dealer and the Company are independent contractors; neither is the other's agent. The Company may refer vehicles to other dealers.

14. Term and termination. One year, renewing automatically unless either party gives 30 days' written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for breach of Sections 3, 7, 9, or 10. Obligations to owners on vehicles already accepted survive termination.

15. Confidentiality and data. Owner information is confidential, used only for the referred transaction, kept no longer than required by law, and deleted or returned on request when the transaction is complete.

16. Governing law. Florida law; venue Miami-Dade County, Florida.
